02 — What it does

Ten apps,
one screen.

Build pages from over forty widgets. Put your portfolio next to your training diary, or your parcels next to the weather. It's your dashboard, so it's arranged your way.

01

Money

Live portfolio and positions, net worth across accounts, statement analysis, and spending you log by voice — geotagged onto a map, deducted from the right pot.

02

Health

Apple Health rings, WHOOP recovery, and training sessions logged with perceived exertion and how they actually felt, kept as a diary.

03

Food

Say a whole day — "porridge for breakfast, katsu curry for dinner" — and it estimates macros against targets worked out from your own numbers.

04

Research

Nightly, from official SEC filings: which holdings report soon, how their fundamentals are trending, and an analyst you can question.

05

The Logger

Press your Action Button and talk. Atlas works out whether it was a purchase, a meal, a workout or a note, and files it accordingly.

06

Everything else

Parcels, trains, aircraft overhead, server uptime, infrastructure costs, hour-by-hour weather, calendar, tasks, notes, habits — and three games.

03 — Built differently

Bring your own keys.

Atlas reads from Trading 212, WHOOP, 17TRACK and others using credentials you provide. That means no subscription funding a middleman, no company sitting between you and your own data, and nothing to breach. It also means Atlas keeps working exactly as well whether one person uses it or a million do.
