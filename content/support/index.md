## Common Questions

### Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. Kestra has no accounts and no servers of mine — nothing is uploaded, and no analytics or tracking of any kind is collected. API keys are held in the iOS Keychain, encrypted by the system. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) for detail.

### A widget says it needs a key or a server. Why?

Some widgets read from services you already use — Trading 212, WHOOP, 17TRACK and so on. Because Kestra talks to them directly from your phone rather than through a middleman, you supply your own key. Everything else works without any setup.

### How do I try Kestra without connecting anything?

**Settings → Data → Fill with sample data.** Every widget populates with realistic figures so you can see how it all works. Erase everything from the same screen to return to a clean install.

### What does Kestra Pro include?

A one-off purchase — not a subscription — unlocking Earnings Radar, the AI daily briefing, all seven themes and push notifications. Everything else stays free permanently, including the full portfolio, health, spending, training and parcel tracking.

### I paid but Pro isn't showing

**Settings → Kestra Pro → Restore Purchase.** Make sure you're signed into the same Apple Account used for the purchase. If it still doesn't appear, email me with your purchase date.

### How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by me. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and sign in with your Apple Account.

### Dictation isn't picking up what I say

Check **Settings → Kestra** on your iPhone and confirm both **Microphone** and **Speech Recognition** are allowed. Dictation works best away from background noise. If a food or amount isn't recognised, you can always type it instead.

### Are the calorie figures accurate?

They're estimates, shown as a range rather than a single number precisely because they're approximate. Treat them as a guide, not a measurement. Kestra is not a medical device and gives no medical advice.

### Is any of the financial information advice?

No. Kestra displays figures and public regulatory filings so you can see them in one place. Nothing in it is financial advice or a recommendation, and it isn't authorised or regulated by the FCA. Always verify against your broker before acting on anything.

### Can I rearrange the layout?

Yes. **Settings → Pages & Widgets → Edit**, then drag the handles to reorder or swipe to remove. You can add, rename, reorder and delete whole pages from the same screen.

### How do I back up my data?

Because everything is local, Kestra data is included in your normal iPhone backup — iCloud Backup or a computer backup will carry it to a new device. Deleting the app deletes its data permanently, and I hold no copy.

## Reporting a Bug

Useful reports usually mention:

- What you were doing when it happened
- What you expected, and what happened instead
- Whether it happens every time or occasionally
- Your iPhone model and iOS version
- A screenshot, if there's anything visible

Feature suggestions are welcome too — a lot of what's in Kestra started as someone's passing idea.
