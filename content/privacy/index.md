## Overview

Atlas is a personal life dashboard built on one principle: your data belongs to you. Everything you enter into Atlas stays on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any server we operate.

## What Data Atlas Uses

With your explicit permission, Atlas may access the following — only on your device:

- **Health data** (steps, heart rate, sleep, calories) — read from Apple Health, never sent anywhere
- **Location** — used only for local weather via Open-Meteo. Not stored or tracked.
- **Camera** — only when you scan a meal for macro analysis
- **Face ID** — only to unlock the in-app password vault, via Apple's secure APIs
- **Photo library** — only when you choose to analyse a meal photo

None of this data is collected by Atlas or its developer.

## Financial Data

If you connect a brokerage account (e.g. Trading 212), your API credentials are stored exclusively in your device's encrypted Keychain. They are never sent to or stored on any server operated by Atlas.

## Personal Server (Optional)

Atlas supports an optional personal server for advanced features (Obsidian sync, AI briefing, WHOOP). This is a server you own and operate — Atlas does not provide or manage it.

## Third-Party Services

- **Open-Meteo** (weather) — open-source, no personal data sent
- **Financial Modeling Prep** (market data) — receives stock ticker symbols only
- **WHOOP API** — reads recovery data with your explicit consent only

We use no advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or tracking frameworks of any kind.

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally using iOS standard storage. Sensitive credentials use the iOS Keychain with `WhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly` protection — encrypted at rest, never included in unencrypted backups.

## Children

Atlas is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.
