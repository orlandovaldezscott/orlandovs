## What It Is

Sections is a SwiftUI editor for the carousels, collages and stories people post on Instagram. Instead of editing each slide separately, every slide sits side by side on one long canvas, so a photo or shape can run across the join between two slides — the effect that makes a carousel feel like one continuous image when swiped.

## How It Works

- **Home** — saved projects and a gallery of ready-made layouts
- **Editor** — drag between slides and it snaps to the nearest one; add photos, text and shapes as layers; change the background, aspect ratio and number of slides
- **Export** — each slide exported separately, ready to post in order

## Built For Every Screen

One codebase runs on iPhone, iPad (with multitasking) and Mac via Catalyst, in every orientation. It came out of my photography A-level: I wanted a faster way to lay out a series of images than the tools I was using.
