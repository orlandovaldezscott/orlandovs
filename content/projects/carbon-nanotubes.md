## single walled carbon nanotubes 

There are two different types of carbon nanotubes single wall carbon nanotubes "SWCNTs" these are extremely thin incredibly strong cables made from just one layer of carbon atoms which are then rolled up to form a tube shape, it can then conduct electricity if arranged in a certain way, they are commonly 0.5-1.5 nanometers in diameter.

## multi walled carbon nanotubes 

Multi walled carbon nanotubes MWCNT's these are stacked layers of graphene where each layer is another carbon nanotube making them longer with slightly different properties from SWCNTs this is due to having more layers and can then conduct electricity they are typically greater than 100 nanometers in diameter these can therefore extend to lengths of several micrometers and even millimetres.

Both types of CNTS vary widely depending on their specific structure, whether they are single-walled, how chiral they are in terms of atom arrangement around the circumference.

Carbon nanotubes are amazing candidates for applications in nano electromechanical systems NEMS aswell as electronics, because they allow electricity to flow with less resistance than copper which is most commonly used in electronics, hence an example would be a computer that starts quicker and gets less hot, this aswell as energy storage has huge potential for long term benefits
