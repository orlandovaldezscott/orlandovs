Atlas was the first name for the app that is now [Kassi](/projects/kassi/). I started it on 12 June 2026 and had a working version by the next day: a personal dashboard for iPhone where everything is a widget, split across three pages called Life, Business and Finance.

The logo was a wireframe globe. An atlas holds the whole world in one book, and the app was meant to hold my whole day on one screen.

In late July I redesigned it from scratch. The rule was "built, not decorated": near-black background, warm off-white text, one acid green accent, a serif for anything human and monospace for anything measured. Nothing rounded anywhere.

It was renamed Kestra on 31 July, then Kassi in September. The name and the globe went. Most of the design stayed.
