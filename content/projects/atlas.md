## What Atlas Is

Atlas is a native iOS app (SwiftUI) that replaces the dozen apps you check every day with one calm, minimalist dashboard. Weather, calendar, health, medication, meals, gym, tasks, parcels, notes and a full live investment portfolio — all on pages **you** design.

The thesis: most screen time is fragmentation. Ten apps, ten feeds, ten sets of notifications. Atlas collapses them into a single glanceable surface with **zero feeds and zero distractions** — so you check it, get what you need, and put the phone down.

## The Widget System

Everything in Atlas is a widget. Users build their own app:

- **Add, rename, delete and reorder pages** on the nav bar — choose any name and icon
- **15 widgets** to place anywhere: Weather (7-day swipe), Calendar, Health, Medication tracker, Macro tracker, Sport & Gym, Tasks, Parcels, Notes, Net Worth, Trading 212 portfolio, Portfolio Analysis, and more
- Layouts persist, so the app becomes genuinely yours — a trader's Atlas looks nothing like a student's

## What It Solves

- **Phone addiction** — one app, no feeds. Atlas even learns your usage patterns and tells you when you reach for it most
- **Financial blindness** — live Trading 212 portfolio with movers, sort filters and an on-device analysis engine that flags concentration risk and drawdowns (observations, never advice). Net worth aggregates investments, cash and bank automatically
- **Scattered health data** — Apple Health, medication ticking, meal macros and gym streaks in one place, with a profile that computes BMI and will drive training recommendations
- **Notes friction** — write a note in Atlas, it lands in your Obsidian vault automatically

Plus a hidden games drawer (a 6-level chess engine with capture animations, and 2048) — because one game is healthier than one feed.
